Founder-Managed Health-Tech Startup

Building Closed-Loop Workforce Systems During Rapid Hypergrowth

The Challenge: A fast-growing, founder-managed health-tech startup needed an end-to-end people infrastructure to navigate a 400% headcount expansion while keeping teams aligned and reducing retention risk during high-stakes scaling phases.

The Strategy: Designed a structured change-management communication plan and coached leadership on transparent communication across all business lines. Increased structured 1:1 check-ins and built corporate goals that directly fed into a new performance management framework—creating a full closed-loop workforce cycle.

The Outcome: Executed the hiring roadmap through 400% expansion while keeping key talent attrition under 5%. Increased manager confidence, created expectation clarity, and established operational predictability across the organization.

Global Clinical Research Organization

Global Talent Architecture & International Center of Excellence Build

The Challenge: A North American clinical research organization with 450 employees and 1,250 consultants needed to optimize operating costs, transition away from costly Employer of Record (EOR) models, and build a sustainable talent pipeline.

The Strategy: Led organizational change management to expand operations into India, establishing a low-cost Center of Excellence (CoE) to drive primary cost savings. Successfully managed Canadian EOR transitions to launch permanent employment infrastructure and designed a year-long rotational Talent Development program to address industry-wide talent shortages.

The Outcome: Achieved an 85% increase in gross profitability while building a predictable, long-term talent pipeline and establishing operational foundations across international jurisdictions and acquisitions.

Clinical-Stage Biopharmaceutical

Local Sourcing Architecture & Business-Led Engagement

The Challenge: A clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company operating in a specialized GMP environment faced high costs associated with relocating talent from Bay Area hubs, while needing to maintain team stability during market volatility.

The Strategy: Shifted away from expensive Bay Area relocation models by establishing local talent partnerships with Texas A&M University and regional institutions to train and hire specialized manufacturing personnel. Sponsored and supported business-led DE&I and employee engagement committees to connect everyday roles directly to overarching business goals.

The Outcome: Significantly reduced relocation expenses and achieved under 5% annual turnover through high-impact employee engagement and clear internal promotion pathways.
