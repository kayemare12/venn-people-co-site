Who We Are

Where executive-level HR strategy meets genuine, human support. Venn People Co. was founded to bridge the gap between what organizations need to scale and what people need to thrive.

Our Story

Why We Started

After years of leadership experience in people operations, change management, talent acquisition, and learning and development inside hypergrowth environments, I realized something fundamental: traditional corporate structures often force a choice between business strategy and human empathy.

Venn People Co. was founded to allow us to work directly with the clients, founders, teams, and individuals that matter. We aim to make a tangible difference in the workplace—building systems that scale without losing sight of the people who run them.

At its core, "Venn" represents the intersection where strategic business objectives and human potential meet. That intersection is where we operate every day.
